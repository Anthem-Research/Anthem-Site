Research brief / 17 November 2026 / v0.1

ARENA

Bounded physical coordination: institutional boundaries before digital governance.

Research proposition

ARENA examines whether small, voluntary physical collaborations can reduce institutional ambiguity by defining place, purpose, authority, obligations, records, disputes and exit before introducing digital governance mechanisms. A registry may make agreed states inspectable; it cannot establish their legal or empirical truth.

Research question

What minimum institutional structure permits a bounded physical project to coordinate people and resources while limiting authority drift, uneven contribution, dispute costs and practical barriers to exit?

Minimum institutional specification

Registry and trust boundary

The experimental, tokenless registry model can record an Arena identifier, charter hash, operator address, protocol version, lifecycle status and module references. It does not prove property title, genuine consent, contribution truth, legal enforceability, fair adjudication or physical safety. Sensitive records and decision narratives remain off-chain.

Illustrative test case

Hypothetical only: a coastal field station hosts one steward, three researchers and two equipment operators during a twelve-week observation campaign. In week five, a researcher disputes exclusion from a shared sensor; the steward cites a calibration issue. A pre-agreed procedure would collect schedules, calibration notes and communication records, assign a non-conflicted reviewer, and document an outcome and appeal window. This is not a deployed ARENA.

Evaluation method

Compare real charters and operator manuals; extract recurring decisions and edge cases; apply an ARENA-aligned schema to two hypothetical environments; run tabletop scenarios on membership, authority, contributions, disputes, emergency powers and exit; record ambiguity, exceptions and residual legal dependencies. Criteria include authority clarity, contribution records, dispute handling, practical exit costs and operator concentration.

Potential commissioned application

With a research partner examining conditional agreements or institutional coordination: map delegation and agreement lifecycles; specify evidence and records; test revocation, review and exit through adversarial scenarios; deliver a decision-rights model, evidence schema and implementation-facing design memo. Scope, access and fee would be agreed for a concrete project.

Current status and limits

ARENA exists as authored protocol specifications, charter and recognition models, operator guidance, dispute payloads, a registry whitepaper and experimental Solidity reference contracts. No operating Arena, legal approval, security audit, empirically measured improvement or independently validated deployment is established. The development repository remains private. The material presented here is research, not a legal opinion, security assessment or operating certification.
