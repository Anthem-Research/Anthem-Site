ARENA

Research on bounded physical coordination: how sites, vessels, studios, field stations, and other shared environments make authority, participation, responsibility, and exit explicit.

ARENA starts with an operational boundary and a defined objective. Its institutional model specifies a steward, membership, contributions, custody and records, dispute procedures, renewal, exit, and dissolution.

An optional recognition registry could record charter versions, operator addresses, lifecycle status, and module references. It does not establish genuine consent, lawful authority, safety, or compliance. Sensitive records remain off-chain.

The work comprises protocol specifications, charter and recognition standards, operator guidance, registry architecture, and experimental reference contracts. No deployed Arena, audited codebase, or independently validated result is claimed.

The proposed next step is a bounded comparative study and tabletop evaluation of authority, evidence, dispute, and exit scenarios, followed by a consent-based field pilot if appropriate.
