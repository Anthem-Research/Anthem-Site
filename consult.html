Commissions

Anthem undertakes selected independent research and design projects across institutional systems, physical environments, computational methods, and field research.

Research studies: a defined question, evidence review, comparative analysis, systems mapping, and a written synthesis.

Institutional design: roles, authority, contribution, custody, records, review, disputes, renewal, and exit.

Field systems: observation methods, site records, evidence structures, interfaces, and evaluation frameworks.

Work is scoped around a clear question, agreed deliverables, timescale, and fee. Research and experimental prototypes are not offered as legal, safety, or security certifications.
