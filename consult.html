Independent Systems Studio / Selected work

Commissions

Anthem initiates independent research and undertakes selected commissioned studies and design projects. Work begins with a defined question, documented assumptions, a clear method and agreed deliverables. Specialist collaborators are engaged where a project requires them.

01 / Research Study

Structured investigation of an institutional, material, ecological or computational question. Typical outputs: evidence register, comparative analysis, system diagrams, a written synthesis and a decision memo.

Illustrative duration: approximately 15 working days. Scope and fee by proposal.

02 / Institutional Design Sprint

Operating and governance design for a shared site, research programme or bounded collaboration. Typical outputs: stakeholder and authority maps, charter outline, contribution and custody rules, review and exit flows, records specification and scenario tests.

Illustrative duration: approximately 20 working days. Scope and fee by proposal.

03 / Field System Study

Observation and evidence methods for a bounded site or programme. Typical outputs: field protocol, sampling or observation plan, data schema, site register, mapping/interface concept and evaluation criteria.

Illustrative duration: 15–25 working days. Scope and fee by proposal.

Work and research

The ARENA research brief demonstrates current institutional and protocol-design methods. Anatomy of the Network is an independent research essay. Project examples are research and design work, not claims of previous client delivery.

Enquiries

Send a short description of the question, operating context, intended output, timeframe and available project budget to hello@anthemresearch.xyz.

Anthem does not provide legal opinions, regulated ecological certification, production security audits or other professional sign-off without appropriately qualified partners. Experimental software and research models are not presented as validated production deployments.
